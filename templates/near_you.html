Mpumalanga Skills Development Zone Officially Launched

Nkangala, Mpumalanga | 29 October 2026

The Minister of Higher Education and Training, Mr Buti Manamela, together with the Premier of Mpumalanga, Mr Mandla Ndlovu, officially launched the Mpumalanga Skills Development Zone (SDZ) on 29 October 2026 in Nkangala, Mpumalanga, as part of the Just Energy Transition (JET) Skills Ecosystem.

This historic milestone marks a significant step towards developing a skilled, capable, and future-ready workforce to support a just and inclusive energy transition. The Mpumalanga SDZ aims to strengthen skills development, align training with emerging economic opportunities, and prepare communities for the changing energy landscape.

The launch reinforces the importance of collaboration between government, skills development institutions, industry, and local communities to ensure that workers and communities are equipped to participate in the opportunities created by the transition to a sustainable, low-carbon economy.

Building skills, empowering communities, and enabling a just energy transition.

Northern Cape Skills Development Zone Officially Launched

Northern Cape | 19 November 2026

The Northern Cape officially launched its Skills Development Zone (SDZ) on 19 November 2026, together with the Just Energy Transition (JET) Skills Ecosystem in the province. The launch is an important step towards strengthening skills development and preparing communities for opportunities in the green economy.

The Honourable Premier, Dr Zamani Saul, told the launch that the Northern Cape is strategically positioned to play a significant role in the green energy transition. He highlighted the province's competitive advantage in green energy, its rich mineral resources and its vast renewable energy potential, and the importance of developing the skills and expertise needed to support its evolving economic landscape.

A panel discussion brought key stakeholders together to exchange insights on skills development and the role of the JET in building a skilled and capable workforce. The discussion stressed collaboration, knowledge-sharing and coordinated efforts to strengthen skills development across the province.

Ms Nadia Starr, Acting Director-General of the Department of Higher Education and Training, emphasised the importance of aligning skills development with industrial investment, so that education and training institutions prepare future talent, employers can access the skills they need, and communities benefit from emerging employment opportunities.

The official signing marks a commitment to building a skilled workforce that will support the province's energy transition and economic growth.

Building skills, empowering communities, and enabling a just energy transition.
