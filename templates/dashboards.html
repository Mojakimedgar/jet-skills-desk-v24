      How the data arrives

      This dashboard depends on data-sharing with SETAs, training providers, employers and workstream partners. Partners report aggregate counts using the standard template above (period, workstream, stage, number of learners, reporting organisation and source). The Desk's harvester also finds learner, intern and graduate figures in public reports, such as JET programme reports. These go to a review queue, and nothing is shown here until a person has checked and approved it. Only aggregate counts are accepted: no names or ID numbers.

        Privacy

        What these dashboards record

        Usage counts (opened synopses, copied references, completed questionnaires) are simple totals. We do not store IP addresses, device details, cookies or anyone's questionnaire answers, and the counts cannot be traced to a person. Occupation and labour-market figures come from official sources cited beside each chart.
