About green hydrogen occupations

Many hydrogen roles do not yet exist as separate occupations in the national classification. A hydrogen skills research report discussed by CHIETA identified 138 roles in the green hydrogen value chain, 77 of them not reflected in the Organising Framework for Occupations, as reported by the Diamond Fields Advertiser. See the LMI research publication for the full analysis.

Occupation names, OFO codes and minimum qualifications come from the 2024 National List of Occupations in High Demand (Government Gazette 50510). Minimum qualifications are sign-posts for skills planning, not employment requirements. Workstream links and plain-English summaries are indicative drafts and should be confirmed by workstream leads.
